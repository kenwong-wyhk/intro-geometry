5.3 | Check your drawing

1

Sketch first, then choose the case.

Write the given measurements on a rough sketch. Is the angle between the two sides? Is the side between the two angles?

2

The opening of the compasses is the radius.

Set it on the ruler, and do not squeeze the compasses while drawing.
