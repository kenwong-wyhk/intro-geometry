5.2 | Classify: answers

A circle is a plane figure, but not a polygon.

Centre $O$: the same distance from every point on the circle

Radius $OP$: from the centre to the circle

Diameter $AB$: across the circle, through the centre

Circumference: the curve itself, and its length

diameter $= 2 \times$ radius
