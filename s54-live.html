5.4 | Sketching a solid

Oblique grid: the front face keeps its true shape, and the depth goes along the slanted lines.

One slanted grid line stands for 2 units.

Isometric grid: no face keeps its true shape, but all three directions follow the grid, one grid line for each unit.
