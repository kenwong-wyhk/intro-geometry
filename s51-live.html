5.1 | Read the marks

$AB \parallel DC$$AD \perp DC$

The answer is C.

$AB$ has the same direction as $DC$, and $AD$ meets $DC$ at $90^\circ$. So $AD$ meets $AB$ at $90^\circ$ too.

5.1 | Read the marks: answers

1

Name an angle with three letters.

$\angle ABC$: the vertex is the middle letter. $\angle B$ is unclear when several angles meet at $B$.

2

Know $90^\circ$, $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$.

Right, straight and round angles. Complementary angles add up to $90^\circ$; supplementary angles add up to $180^\circ$.

3

Use the marks and the numbers, not how it looks.

Arrows for parallel, a small square for perpendicular. Add the angles before saying a line is straight.
